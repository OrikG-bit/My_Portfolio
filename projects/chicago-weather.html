Chicago Weather & Transportation

I’m looking at how daily ridership on Chicago Transit Authority (CTA) buses and trains varies with temperature, rain, and snow.

From hours to days

CTA reports ridership by day. The weather records contain 146,448 hourly observations. Grouping those observations by Chicago local date produced 6,101 complete days to compare with ridership.

The 2019 cold snap

The transportation notebook looks at the week of January 27 to February 2, 2019. My next step is to match weather and ridership by date to see how travel during that cold snap compares with other days.
