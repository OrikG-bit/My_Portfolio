PeerSpace

“Who can I ask for help?” I built PeerSpace to give first-year students at National Louis University a place to start with questions like this.As a peer mentor, I built a campus guide around questions first-year students ask.

Start with the question

A first-year student may know they need help before they know the name of the right office. I organized PeerSpace around decisions students face, like finding someone to talk to or making room to study during a busy week.

From course topics to campus resources

I drew on selected GEN 103 topics, including support networks, study habits, and belonging. I paired short guidance with official university links so students can read an explanation and go straight to the source.

I chose paper-cut illustrations to give each topic a recognizable image and make this independent, student-led guide feel welcoming.
