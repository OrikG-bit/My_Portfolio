The problem

A Linux workstation was detecting only three of its four NVIDIA GPUs. The task was to work through the detection issue and confirm that the system could recognize all four.

My approach

  • Started with Bash-based troubleshooting and system diagnostics.
  • Moved to a physical inspection when the software checks did not resolve the issue.
  • Inspected the GPU, cables, and connections, working with the Strata team.
  • Confirmed that all four GPUs were ultimately visible in the system.

The result

The system ultimately recognized all four GPUs. I personally contributed both command-line diagnostics and physical hardware inspection during the investigation.

What I learned

A useful troubleshooting process crosses the boundary between software and hardware. Moving methodically from command-line checks to physical inspection helped me build a more systematic approach to an unfamiliar problem.
