The opportunity

The project explored how speech analytics could support work around cognitive health. I proposed an initiative by reviewing research, identifying business applications, and presenting the concept to leadership. It was approved and contributed to a research grant proposal.

My contribution

I contributed across the MVP, with my main focus on n8n workflow automation and rapid risk analysis. The audio-processing work brought together Python, FastAPI, OpenSMILE, and an existing pre-trained Nuroxa model. I also labeled and validated audio segments to support analysis and model-related development.

What I delivered

I delivered an end-to-end MVP in five weeks and labeled and validated more than 1,000 audio segments. The work connected research, audio processing, an existing model, and a repeatable automation workflow.

Scope and learning

The prototype applied an existing pre-trained model to the workflow; its outputs still require further validation for clinical use. The experience taught me how to balance technical and product decisions within an internship timeline.

From speech to features

Tempo, rhythm and pauses describe different aspects of a recording. In my openSMILE workflow, the starting point is a table of acoustic features already extracted from the audio.
