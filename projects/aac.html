AAC donation email automation

I designed a donation follow-up workflow for the Azerbaijani American Community (AAC). It starts with an email, adds the donation details to a spreadsheet, and sends the donor a thank-you.

Role
Workflow designer
Organization
Azerbaijani American Community
Tools & methods
n8n, Gmail, Google Sheets, JavaScript

From inbox to donation log

I wanted one incoming message to take care of two tasks: recording a donation and acknowledging the donor. I used Gmail as the trigger and wrote a JavaScript step to check the message text for donation keywords and extract details such as the donor’s name and email address.

When to send a thank-you

A decision step checks whether the email matched the donation rules. On a match, the next node appends a row to Google Sheets. Only after that step succeeds does Gmail send the thank-you. Other messages stop at the decision step.
